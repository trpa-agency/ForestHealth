At a glance

Structure of the Basin's conifer forest

Forest structure describes how old a stand is, how much of the ground it shades, and how crowded it is. The program tracks three measures across the non-urban conifer forest of the Basin: seral stage, canopy cover, and stand density. All three come from 30-meter remote sensing products and are summarized by forest type, so each type is compared with the structure it evolved under rather than with a single basin-wide number.

Share of each forest type in the five seral stage and canopy cover classes, and the share of acres at or below the desired stand density for their type and stage. Both are from the 2023 assessment, which covers disturbance through 2022.

Monitoring approach

How forest structure is measured

Wall-to-wall remote sensing at 30-meter resolution, summarized by forest type. Ground plots and LiDAR will calibrate the modeled products as they come online.

Source data

Sierra Nevada Regional Resource Kit (California Wildfire and Forest Resilience Task Force, 2024), built on the F3 forest structure framework. 2023 vintage, covering disturbance through 2022. Vegetation types from the California Wildlife Habitat Relationships system.

Rasters are clipped to the TRPA boundary and to non-urban land use, then tabulated by forest type.

Assessed extent

115,396 acres of conifer forest, about 56 percent of the Basin's land area. Excludes designated wilderness, urban land use (mixed-use, residential, and tourist), and non-conifer types. Of that, 111,424 acres carry seral stage data.

Frequency and partners

Assessed each threshold evaluation cycle from the current Regional Resource Kit release. Developed with the Tahoe Fire and Fuels Team and peer reviewed by the Tahoe Science Advisory Council, which recommended pairing the modeling with on-the-ground monitoring.

Field calibration

Ground plots and LiDAR

Modeled structure is only as good as its calibration. A basin-wide plot network is in design to measure trees per acre, basal area, diameter, and canopy cover on the ground and compare them with the remote sensing products that cover the whole forest.

About the program. The Forest Structure monitoring program tracks seral stage, canopy cover, and stand density under the Vegetation Preservation threshold category. It supports two threshold standards: Seral Stage and Canopy Cover, and Stand Density.
