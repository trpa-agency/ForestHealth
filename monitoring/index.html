Each bar is the share of the assessed area that currently meets the standard. The black tick is the target. For Landscape Fire Dynamics the standard is a ceiling, so the bar shows the share at risk and the tick is the maximum allowed. Subalpine Conifer is a non-degradation standard and has no percentage.

Source: TRPA, Forest Health Threshold Standards report, 2023 assessment.

Data sources. Forest structure and fire hazard layers from the Sierra Nevada Regional Resource Kit (California Wildfire and Forest Resilience Task Force, 2024), F3 framework at 30-meter resolution. Fire hazard modeled by Pyrologix with the Wildfire Exposure Simulation Tool under 90th-percentile fire weather. Forest types from the California Wildlife Habitat Relationships system. Fuels treatment accomplishments from the EIP Project Tracker. These are landscape-scale planning models, not stand-level measurements.
